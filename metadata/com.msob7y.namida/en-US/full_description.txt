A Beautiful and Feature-rich Music & Video Player with YouTube Support, built in Flutter.

Everything you might expect from a music player, plus:

Library & Indexing
* Powerful Indexer & Tag Editor
* Artists and Genres Separators
* Prevent Duplicated Tracks
* Set Minimum File Size & Duration
* Folders-based Library system with exclusion support
* Sort by almost any property of the track or album

Look & Feel
* Material3-like Theme
* Dynamic Theming (player colors picked from current album artwork)
* Home, Tracks, Albums, Artists, Genres, Playlists, Queues and Folders pages
* Waveform Seekbar
* Lots of customizations

Sound
* Parametric Equalizer with AutoEQ import
* Bit-perfect Playback & USB DAC Direct Access
* High precision 32-bit float audio
* Audio Path viewer
* Output Device Selection & Mono Audio
* Crossfade, Gapless Playback, Play/Pause Fade Effect & Skip Silence
* Speed & Pitch Control (semitones & 432Hz) & Loudness Enhancer
* Per-track Sound Settings
* Replay Gain (Normalize Audio)

Streaming & YouTube
* Best Video & Audio Quality
* Audio Only & Data Saver Mode
* Support Downloads, Caching & Offline Playback
* Video View with gesture support
* SponsorBlock & Return YouTube Dislike Integration
* Segments and Heatmap for videos
* Cache Priority System
* Multi Library Support (Local / YT / Subsonic / Jellyfin / WebDAV / SMB)

Others
* Lyrics auto fetching & displaying (Synced & Plain)
* Sleep Timer (Tracks or Minutes)
* Control pausing scenarios
* Search tracks by title, artist, album, filename, even lyrics
* Persistent and reliable queue system
* Reliable History System
* Most Played Playlist
* Custom Playlists with custom artworks
* Auto Import Playlists from M3U files
* Sync between devices over local network

Additional Cool Features
* Smort Tracks Generation (based on listening history)
* Animating Thumbnail (reacts to current audio peak)
* Miniplayer Party Mode
* Particles Effect
* Track Play Mode selection
* Repeat for N times
* Extract feat. & ft. artists
* "Lost Memories" feature
* Import YouTube History, LastFM, Spotify & ListenBrainz

Video Integration
* Full video library indexing & playback
* Videos can be played independently or alongside music
* Dedicated videos folder view

Permissions Explained
* WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK — keep app alive for media playback
* INTERNET — for artist/album images, streaming & downloading
* READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android ≤ 12) — list files from indexer folders only
* READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Android 13+) — list media files from indexer folders
* WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android ≤ 10) — for file operations
* MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 10+) — for editing tags, creating backups, saving artworks, deleting files, exporting playlists, downloading YouTube content
* VIBRATE — haptic feedback
* POST_NOTIFICATIONS — progress notifications
* REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS — improve downloads
* WRITE_SETTINGS — set audio as ringtone
* QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES — open custom equalizer apps

Companion App: Namida Sync

Looking for a seamless way to back up and restore your Namida data and music library? Check out Namida Sync — a companion app built specifically for Namida!

Features:
* One-tap backup & restore for Namida player data and music library
* Google Drive Sync — cloud-based backup with automatic sync
* Local Network Transfer — peer-to-peer sharing without internet
* Smart file handling — incremental sync, skips duplicates, handles large libraries
* Cross-platform compatibility — works between Android and Windows
* Auto-detection — finds your latest backup and music folders automatically
* Folder structure preservation — maintains your music organization during transfers
